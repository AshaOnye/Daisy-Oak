Daisy Oak Foundation

Development that Starts with People

At Daisy Oak Foundation, we believe sustainable development begins by understanding the realities of the people we serve.

We work alongside communities and partners to address interconnected challenges affecting health, nutrition, education, economic opportunity and social inclusion. Our programmes combine practical support with education, community engagement, capacity strengthening, referrals, partnerships and advocacy to create pathways toward healthier, safer and more resilient communities. We do not measure our work only by the number of activities completed or items distributed. We increasingly ask a more important question: what changed for the people we served?

WHAT WE DO

Health & Nutrition

Improving access to practical health information, maternal and child health support, adolescent health interventions, nutrition education and appropriate referral.

Education & Learning

Education & Learning Reducing barriers to learning and supporting vulnerable children and young people with resources, opportunities and community engagement.

Women & Youth Empowerment

Building practical skills, confidence, financial capability and pathways toward sustainable livelihoods for women, youth and the society at large.

Food & Nutrition Security

Supporting vulnerable households, schools and communities while promoting better nutrition knowledge, food access and community resilience.

Protection & Inclusion

Protection & Inclusion Promoting safeguarding, disability inclusion, dignity, rights awareness and support for vulnerable children and populations.

Youth Leadership & Governance

Strengthening young people’s knowledge, leadership and meaningful participation in community and decision-making processes.

Our Approach

Listen. Connect. Deliver. Measure. Sustain

This is the DaisyCARE approach - a practical framework for ensuring that programmes are informed by communities, connected to existing systems, delivered with quality, measured for results and designed with sustainability in mind.

PARTNER WITH US

Local understanding. Shared expertise. Greater impact.

We welcome collaboration with government institutions, UN agencies, INGOs, foundations, companies, academic institutions, professional bodies and civil society organisations seeking credible community-level partnership in Nigeria.
