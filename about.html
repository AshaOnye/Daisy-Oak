WHO WE ARE

Working to Create Healthier, More Inclusive and More Resilient Communities

Daisy Oak Foundation is a Nigerian nonprofit organisation established to improve development outcomes for vulnerable and underserved populations. Legally registered in 2021, we work at the intersection of health, nutrition, education, social protection, economic empowerment and inclusion because the challenges facing families and communities are rarely isolated. A mother’s health can affect a child’s well-being. A family’s income can affect nutrition and school participation. A girl’s access to menstrual products can affect dignity and learning. A young person’s lack of opportunity can shape long-term social and economic vulnerability. For us, effective development means seeing those connections and responding to the whole picture.

OUR VISION

A future where vulnerable and underserved people have equitable opportunities to live with dignity, realise their potential and lead healthy, empowered and fulfilling lives.

OUR MISSION

To work with communities and partners to advance equitable access to health, learning and sustainable livelihoods through inclusive, evidence-informed and locally responsive solutions.

OUR JOURNEY

Reaching People One Community at a Time

Daisy Oak Foundation was legally registered in 2021 with a vision of inspiring hope and creating life-changing opportunities for vulnerable populations. As the organisation grows, we are building stronger technical, programme and institutional systems to deliver sustainable, measurable community impact and to partner effectively with national and international development actors.
