MotherCare Community Outreach in Sabon Gari, Bwari

In Sabon Gari, Bwari Area Council, families spoke to Daisy Oak Foundation about everyday needs that affect health, dignity and learning. The MotherCare Community Outreach was designed to respond to three of them together: maternal health, menstrual health and access to basic learning materials.

The 8 September 2026 outreach targeted 100 community members: 30 pregnant and breastfeeding women, 40 adolescent girls and 30 Primary 5 and 6 pupils. Activities included nutrition education for women, menstrual-health education for girls, mother, baby and adolescent kits, and exercise books for pupils. The approach recognised that a family’s well-being is connected: a mother needs useful health information, a girl needs support to manage menstruation with dignity, and a child needs materials that help them take part in school.

MotherCare is part of Daisy Oak Foundation’s effort to bring practical information and support closer to communities. The programme is intended to help reduce immediate barriers and connect community priorities with partners and services that can contribute to longer-term well-being.
